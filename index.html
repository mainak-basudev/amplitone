Good to know

Questions people ask before booking

You’ll chat with an audiologist about your everyday listening, have your hearing checked, and get honest advice on whether a hearing aid would help. There’s no obligation to buy.
Hearing aids don’t reverse hearing loss, but for many types they’re the most effective way to restore clear communication and quality of life. We’ll always be honest about what to expect.
Every device carries a manufacturer warranty, typically 2–4 years. We also handle cleaning, servicing, repairs and software fine-tuning.
Six main styles by size and placement: BTE, RIC, ITE, ITC, CIC and IIC. Your audiologist will recommend what suits your ears and your hearing.
Yes. We’re an authorised partner of Signia, Phonak, ReSound, Widex, Unitron, Starkey and Oticon, with a team of qualified audiologists.
